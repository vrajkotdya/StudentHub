About StudentHub

StudentHub was created to unify campus communication and student services into one simple, easily accessible online portal. Whether you need to sign up for a technical workshop, review campus notices, or reach out to faculty coordinators, StudentHub brings everything together in one place.

Our Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To foster an active, connected, and informed campus community through accessible and reliable digital tools.

Our Mission

To simplify administrative processes, keep students informed about campus happenings, and provide a transparent platform for student feedback and engagement.

About the College

Founded in 1998, City Engineering & Technology College is committed to academic excellence, innovative research, and vibrant student development. With over 4,000 students across various disciplines, our campus is a lively space for both intellectual pursuit and extracurricular activities.
