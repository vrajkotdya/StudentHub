Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Find quick answers to common queries regarding account creation, event registration, and portal services.

1. Portal Registration & Account Access

Who is eligible to register on StudentHub?

All currently enrolled undergraduate and postgraduate students of CHARUSAT (CSPIT & DEPSTAR institutes) with a valid university student ID number can create an account.

How do I reset my account password if I forget it?

You can use the "Forgot Password" option on the Sign In page, or visit the department network administrator with your student ID card.

Can I update my registered mobile number and email address?

Yes. Once logged in, navigate to Student Profile to submit profile update requests.

2. Campus Events & Hackathon Registrations

Is there a registration fee for departmental technical workshops?

Most internal workshops and guest lectures are free of cost for CHARUSAT students. Specialized national competitions or hackathons may have nominal team registration fees mentioned on the event details page.

Where can I view my registered events and confirmation status?

Your confirmed and pending event registrations appear directly inside your personalized Student Dashboard.

Will I receive certificates for participating in workshops?

Yes. Digital verifiable e-certificates are issued directly to the student portal within 7 business days following the event conclusion.

3. Academic Circulars & Grievance Redressal

How often are circulars and exam schedules updated?

The academic section updates circulars in real-time. Students also receive priority alerts on the dashboard banner for urgent notices.

Can I submit anonymous feedback about course teaching?

Yes. The Student Feedback form provides an option to keep responses confidential for candid academic reviews.

Didn't find your answer?

Reach out directly to our campus support coordinators through the Helpdesk Contact page or email us at support.studenthub@charusat.edu.in.
